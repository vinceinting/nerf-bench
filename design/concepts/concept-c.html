nerf-bench

Concept C: Foam

Leans into the playful name. A soft foam dart for a logo, a warm yellow, rounded shapes. The fun stops at the brand: charts and verdicts stay exact and dry.

Name and logo

Keeps nerf-bench, lowercase, as a friendly wink. The mark is a toy foam dart seen side on: harmless by design, which is the joke. It is the only place the joke appears.

Rationale

A memorable, shareable look helps a small independent site get noticed and remembered. Keeping every playful element on the brand side of a hard line, and none on the data side, lets the tone be friendly without making any single finding feel like a jab at a lab.
