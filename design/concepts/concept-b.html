Driftwatch

Concept B: Public Record

Reads like a well-kept public ledger or a newspaper's data desk. Cool grey-green paper, a deep civic green, a serif that signals the page was written by someone accountable for it.

Name and logo

Proposes Driftwatch in place of nerf-bench: still light, but it names what is measured (drift from launch) rather than implying a lab did something on purpose. The mark is a gauge with a single needle, the green needle being the only colour in it.

Rationale

The public already trusts the look of a careful newspaper chart more than the look of a tech dashboard. A serif for the findings and a sans for the machinery separates what the site says from how it measured it. The name change is offered, not required: it removes the one word in the brand that the verdict rules forbid.
